Words you'll see

Price = chance
A share pays £1 if the event happens and nothing if not. A price of 80p means the market thinks there's about an 80% chance.
Stop-loss
A safety exit. If the price falls 20% below what was paid, the bot sells and takes a small loss rather than risk losing the whole stake. Big news can make prices jump past it.
Cash out
Once a bet reaches 95p the bot sells, locking in most of the win without waiting for the result.
Break-even win rate
How often the bets must win just to not lose money. It equals the average price paid. Beating it is the real goal; a high win rate alone isn't enough.
Paper money
Pretend money. Prices and order books are real, but no real money is bought or sold.

Each bet is 10% of the pot, at most 5 at once and at most 2 on the same topic. Prices come from Polymarket.
